Welcome! My name is Qingxu Yang 杨卿栩

I am an Assistant Professor of Economics at the School of Economics, Fudan University.

I study digital economics and labor economics, with two research agendas. The first examines how AI reshapes expert decision-making and affects welfare. The second investigates how social norms shape human capital investment and fertility.

I received my Ph.D. in Economics from the Guanghua School of Management at Peking University in 2024. From 2024 to 2026, I was a Postdoctoral Fellow at HKU Business School.
