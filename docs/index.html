About me

I am an Assistant Professor at the School of Economics, Fudan University.

I study digital economics and labor economics. My work spans two main lines of research.

The first examines how artificial intelligence changes expert judgment and decision-making, reshapes the relationship between experts and the people they serve, and affects economic welfare.

The second focuses on family economics. I study how social norms and institutions shape marriage, fertility, and investments within families, with particular attention to women's choices, opportunities, and well-being.

I received my Ph.D. in Economics from the Guanghua School of Management at Peking University in 2024. From 2024 to 2026, I was a Postdoctoral Fellow at HKU Business School.
